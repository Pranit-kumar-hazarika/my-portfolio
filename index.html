About me

I move between code, stories, and visuals.

I’m Pranit Kumar Hazarika, a Computer Science student from Assam, India. I build websites, edit videos, make visual content, and enjoy turning rough ideas into something people can feel.

My work sits between logic and creativity. I like solving technical problems, but I also care about how the final result looks, sounds, and tells a story.

Whether I’m coding a project, editing a clip, or creating artwork, I try to make it clear, useful, and personally expressive.
